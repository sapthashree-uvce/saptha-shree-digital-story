# Saptha Shree Digital Story

1. Project Overview

Design and develop a visually stunning, modern, responsive personal portfolio website for Saptha Shree, a second-year B.Tech student pursuing Artificial Intelligence and Data Science at the University of Visvesvaraya College of Engineering (UVCE), Bengaluru.

The portfolio should present her as an aspiring Data Engineer, an enthusiastic technology learner, and a creative problem solver who enjoys building practical solutions.

The website must showcase her education, technical skills, projects, achievements, certifications, extracurricular activities, and interests in an engaging and professional manner.

Main objective: Create a memorable digital identity that reflects both technical capabilities and creative thinking.

2. Design Inspiration and Visual Direction

Use the attached portfolio reference image as the primary design inspiration.

I want a design similar to a premium digital agency or creative developer portfolio.

Design style

Modern, minimal, editorial and sophisticated.

Asymmetrical layouts instead of conventional centred sections.

Large, expressive typography.

Overlapping images and floating visual elements.

Carefully arranged project showcases.

Generous whitespace and clean spacing.

Subtle animations and interactive hover effects.

A polished, professional appearance without looking overly corporate.

Colour palette

Use a consistent colour palette:

Primary background: Off-white / warm white.

Secondary background: Black / charcoal.

Accent colour: Vibrant orange.

Text: Dark charcoal and grey.

Supporting colours: Light grey and muted neutral shades.

Orange should be used strategically for highlighted words, buttons, important labels, and interactive elements.

Avoid excessive colours, gradients, unnecessary decorations, and overly bright backgrounds.

Typography

Use a combination of:

Bold modern sans-serif fonts for headings.

Elegant italic serif fonts for selected highlighted words.

Clean, readable sans-serif fonts for descriptions.

Headings should be large and expressive, similar to the reference website.

Give every section its own visual identity while maintaining overall consistency.

Use actual project-related images or appropriate visual placeholders.

Do not use random stock photos that do not relate to my projects.

Do not invent professional experience, internships, achievements, project results, statistics or certifications.

Keep the language simple, authentic and professional.

Ensure that the website feels premium, creative and polished.
